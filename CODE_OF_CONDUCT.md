# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must make participation safe, respectful, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, sexualized behavior, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Keep reports, logs, and examples free of secrets and personal information.
- Accept clear correction and maintainer decisions.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the `keton-id/maintainers` team. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, release coordination, and project spaces operated for `probelm`.
